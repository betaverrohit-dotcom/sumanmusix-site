Behind the name

Not just music.
A little piece of life.

SumanMusix is the creative home of Suman Biswas — an independent lyricist, composer and music artist inspired by love, longing, memory and the quiet poetry of everyday life.

Rooted in Bengali emotion and shaped by a cinematic sensibility, the work moves between heartfelt songs, original writing and photography. Every project starts with one question: what should someone feel when the last note fades?
